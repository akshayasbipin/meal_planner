* **Weekly planner:** Builds a varied plan from dishes in the Excel workbook. Breakfast is fixed; lunch, dinner, and snacks are selected automatically.
* **Meal rules:** Favors familiar, easier dishes; uses nutrient priorities and targets from the workbook; avoids same-day and back-to-back meal repeats.
* **Plan controls:** Change meals, keep selected days during regeneration, and save or reopen weekly plans.
* **Shopping + Pantry:** Compares required ingredients with pantry stock, tracks purchases, and lets you update Pantry.
* **Meal prep:** Shows daily meals and ingredients, items to buy, items to check, and dishes with missing ingredient details. Download the report or save it to Excel.
* **Workbook-first:** Edit dishes, settings, and nutrient priorities in Excel. The app currently works without an LLM.
